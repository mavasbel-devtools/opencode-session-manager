## 4. Findings that are not fixed by this patch

### 4.1 C5: a window smaller than the fixed part cannot be rescued

With a 20,000 window the log says `emergency tiered drop skipped: fixed floor ≈41804 already
exceeds ceiling 2340` in both variants, and every request is over the window. The patch makes the
failure visible (provider error or toast) but cannot make it fit. The S7 decision holds: the
reason is shown, it is not silent. Remedy for the user: pick a model whose window is larger than
the fixed part, or `/ctx-flush` / `/clear`.

### 4.2 W4 / issue #828: the stale-reading loop was not reproduced

In `refusal-then-healthy-historian` the unpatched copy also recovers on the first turn after the
historian becomes healthy; after a refusal both variants store `last_context_percentage = 0`
and `last_input_tokens = 0`. So these cases do not show the loop described in
[cortexkit/magic-context#828](https://github.com/cortexkit/magic-context/issues/828), and they do
not prove that the re-measure site is needed. They do prove it causes no regression: all patched
results equal the unpatched ones except the added toasts and the longer historian wait.
Not verified: the exact conditions of the #828 loop (the maintainer's comment of 2026-11-01).
