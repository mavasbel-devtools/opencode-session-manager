## Stores

`VACUUM INTO` copies of this machine's `opencode.db` + `context.db` and of the live tier's second
profile (`~/.cache/opencode-session-manager/live/other-profile`), each migrated by OpenCode 2.0.32
in a sandbox (`opencode session list --standalone`, every `XDG_*` folder and `HOME` temporary):
both end at migration `20261010180456_project_time_archived`, `pragma integrity_check` ok, 338 and
278 sessions kept.

On migrating, 2.0.32 archived (set `project.time_archived`) 23 of 43 projects — every one whose
folder no longer exists (Windows-era `~/AppData/…` and `~/Dev/AIML/…` folders, the emptied
`~/Documents/Work/ITAM/Cursos/*`, `~/Dev/repos/inference-router`, `/tmp/opencode/opttest/proj2`).
From the 2.0.32 binary (`Project.sweep`, `Project.resolve`): the sweep archives a project whose
`worktree` is missing and none of whose recorded folders exists (a recorded folder without a
strategy that exists becomes its `worktree` instead); archived projects are left out of the project
list; resolving any folder of the project again clears `time_archived`, and resolving a project
whose stored `worktree` no longer exists sets it to the resolved folder. Sessions are not changed.
